Every spreadsheet, CSV or tab-delimited file attached to an NCI-funded open-access paper from 2016 to 2022 whose paper cites no data repository. 15,458 files from 4,759 papers, none listed in any dataset catalogue. Search, filter, open the file. Files marked verified dataset were opened and confirmed to hold a real data table; the rest are unverified attachments.

"Open file" goes to the file on PubMed Central; "All files (zip)" downloads the paper's complete supplementary set from Europe PMC. Licenses are the article's as tagged by Europe PMC; check the article before reusing. Nothing here was uploaded or modified by us.
